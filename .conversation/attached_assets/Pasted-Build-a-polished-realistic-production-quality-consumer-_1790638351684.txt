Build a polished, realistic, production-quality consumer insurance website for **Uplift Insurance**.

The purpose is to demonstrate Uplift's future digital quote-to-bind experience to potential insurance carrier and technology partners.

This is currently a prototype with NO real insurance carrier API connected. Use mock data and mock quote/bind services behind the scenes, but **do not clutter the customer-facing experience with "DEMO" labels, prototype warnings, or technical language.**

The website should look and behave like a real insurance shopping experience.

IMPORTANT:

* Do not actually issue insurance.
* Do not process real payments.
* Do not represent mock quotes as actual quotes outside the prototype.
* Keep all mock/API logic behind the UI.
* Structure the code so the mock services can later be replaced with real carrier/API integrations.
* Do not use real carrier logos unless properly licensed.
* Use configurable carrier names so they can easily be replaced later.
